## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an atmospheric, instrument-grade computing environment designed for high-agency individuals who interact continuously with private intelligence. It rejects the hyper-saturated neon glows, generic cool-gray SaaS templates, and sterile dark modes common in synthetic AI software. Instead, it draws structural cues from physical laboratory apparatus, fine horology, archival warm paper, and patinated metals.

The visual direction combines **Tactile / Skeuomorphic** discipline with modern **Minimalist** clarity:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep blackened graphite, warm drafting ink, weathered bronze, and sea glass mineral tones. It evokes an executive workstation or an archival physical console rather than a transient web application.
- **Physicality:** Surfaces have deliberate mass, milled edges, and tactile feedback. Boundaries are delineated by ultra-fine warm paper hairlines rather than diffused neon glows.
- **Restraint Rule:** Maintain a strict 92% calm substrate ratio (warm ink, oxidized stone, low-luminance graphite) with only 8% allocated to functional mineral accents. Accents never compete; each conveys a dedicated cognitive state.

## Colors

The palette operates under low visual glare, balancing long-session ergonomics with immediate semantic comprehension.

### Base Substrates & Ink
- **Canvas Base:** `#0E0F12` (Deep warm ink) – unyielding, non-reflective baseline.
- **Substrate Layers:** Layer 1 (`#121418`), Layer 2 (`#16191F`).
- **Surface Elevation:** Stone base (`#1A1D24`), Raised tactile plate (`#21252E`), Active focal container (`#282D37`).
- **Hairline Dividers:** Muted warm bone hairlines (`rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.08)`) and high-definition architectural borders (`rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.14)`).

### Typography Scale
- **Primary Bone:** `#EDE8DF` – warm unbleached paper tint, avoiding eye fatigue caused by harsh `#FFFFFF`.
- **Secondary Warm Fog:** `#9E988F` – desaturated mineral stone for secondary content and descriptors.
- **Tertiary Smoke:** `#66625C` – subdued slate tone for metadata, unit labels, and structural frames.

### Functional Mineral Accents
- **Primary / Machine Intelligence:** Oxidized Teal (`#3A7D7E`), Deep Wash (`rgba(58, 125, 126, 0.14)`), Highlight Peak (`#5E9FA0`). Denotes active inference, synthesis, and machine intuition.
- **Secondary / Memory & Provenance:** Aged Brass / Antique Gold (`#C4A462`), Wash (`rgba(196, 164, 98, 0.12)`). Used for pinned concepts, associative recall, and institutional knowledge.
- **Tertiary / Attention & Critical Delta:** Burnished Copper / Ember Rust (`#C85A3B`), Soft Wash (`rgba(200, 90, 59, 0.14)`). Marks critical alerts, overdue loops, or real-time interrupts.
- **System Stability & Vitality:** Muted Moss Sage (`#4E7A62`), Wash (`rgba(78, 122, 98, 0.14)`). Designates nominal processing, background sanity, and steady state.
- **Context & Temporal Index:** Mineral Slate-Cyan (`#466070`). Marks timeline navigation, schedules, and archive hierarchies.

## Typography

The typography reinforces the feel of a precision drafting instrument:
- **Headlines and Body:** Rendered in **IBM Plex Sans**. It provides humanist warmth anchored by rationalist engineering roots, producing clean readability on graphite backdrops.
- **Technical Metadata, Status, and Telemetry:** Handled by **Space Mono**. Monospaced styling is enforced for execution timers, model latencies, hex addresses, parameter tokens, and system counters.
- **Tabular Figures:** Always enable OpenType tabular numbers (`tnum`) across all typography roles to prevent jitter when streaming tokens or metrics change dynamically.
- **Contrast & Hierarchy:** Main copy uses bone off-white (`#EDE8DF`), supporting annotations transition to warm fog (`#9E988F`), and operational labels sit quietly in smoke (`#66625C`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an architectural modular grid reminiscent of precision industrial dashboards.

### Grid & Composition
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column modular grid with `gutter` (1rem / 16px) and external `margin` (2rem / 32px). Max content span constraints prevent expansive eye-travel during text generation workflows.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column flexible grid with collapsible inspection sidecars.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column column structure with tight edge constraints (`margin-mobile`: 1rem) and compact internal gaps (`gutter-mobile`: 0.75rem).

### Layout Execution
- Layouts rely on structural pane docks (e.g., Command Deck, Workspace Canvas, Memory Ledger) separated by 1px warm bone borders.
- Density is deliberately compact to medium; visual structure is established through hairline segmentation and balanced micro-paddings (`space-sm`, `space-md`), preserving spatial density without claustrophobia.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use diffuse, colorful drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **structural material layering, top-edge specular highlights, and physical bevels**.

### Physical Bevels & Hairline Frameworks
- Every card and dock utilizes an inner 1px structural hairline: `inset 0 1px 0 0 rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.08)`. This simulates light catching the milled top bevel of an oxidized aluminum or stone plate.
- Outer borders use thin, disciplined contours: `1px solid rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.08)` for dormant panels, increasing to `rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.16)` on active interaction.

### Surface Tiering
- **Floor (`#0E0F12`):** Primary chassis background; receives sunken panels and system gutters.
- **Base Plate (`#16191F`):** Anchored decks, toolbars, and primary workspace panels.
- **Raised Module (`#21252E`):** Movable instrument cards, prompt modules, and dialogue nodes.
- **Tactile Floating Layer (`#282D37`):** Command palettes, context menus, and contextual overlays.

### Shadow Profile
- Shadows act as tight, ambient occlusion contact zones rather than ambient atmospheric glows:
  - **Docked:** `0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`
  - **Elevated/Floating:** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`

## Shapes

The system implements a tightly controlled **Soft (`1`)** corner geometry:
- Standard components (buttons, input boxes, inline pills) adopt a 0.25rem (4px) corner radius.
- Larger cards, modular panels, and flyouts adopt `rounded-lg` at 0.5rem (8px).
- Modal viewports and major canvas splitters peak at `rounded-xl` at 0.75rem (12px).
- Completely round pill shapes (`rounded-full`) are strictly prohibited, except for single-character notification pips or microscopic hardware indicator lights (e.g., 6px status LED dots).

## Components

### Buttons & Trigger Controls
- **Primary Action (Teal Stone):** Background `#3A7D7E`, foreground `#EDE8DF`. Features an inner upper bevel `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.18)` and an outer border `1px solid #2B5E60`. On press, slight micro-inset displacement (`transform: translateY(1px)`).
- **Secondary / Subdued:** Background `#21252E`, hairline border `1px solid rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.12)`, text `#9E988F`. Hover transforms text to `#EDE8DF` and border to `rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.22)`.
- **Destructive / Interrupt:** Background `rgba(200, 90, 59, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(200, 90, 59, 0.3)`, text `#C85A3B`.

### Input Fields & Terminal Consoles
- **Field Canvas:** `#121418` base plate with an inset recessed border: `box-shadow: inset 0 1px 3px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`. Border is `1px solid rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.10)`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#3A7D7E` with no diffuse glow—only a clean `1px solid #3A7D7E` perimeter and subtle specular top hairline.
- **Prefix / Suffix Tokens:** Set in `Space Mono` (`label-md`), tinted in `#66625C`.

### Cards & Modules
- Structured in `#1A1D24` with `0.25rem` corners and a 1px border `rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.08)`.
- Headers are delineated from card bodies using a horizontal hairline divider and an uppercase `Space Mono` category badge (`label-sm`).

### Chips, Badges & Status Indicators
- **AI Intelligence Badge:** `#16191F` background, text `#5E9FA0`, 1px border `rgba(58, 125, 126, 0.35)`.
- **Memory Node:** `#16191F` background, text `#D4B776`, 1px border `rgba(196, 164, 98, 0.35)`.
- **System Vitality Pip:** A 6px physical circular LED indicator with solid moss fill (`#4E7A62`) paired with an inner shadow to simulate an illuminated physical dial.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Square housings (`0.25rem` radius) filled with `#121418` and framed by `rgba(215, 205, 190, 0.18)`.
- Selected state activates a tactile oxidized teal (`#3A7D7E`) fill, inset checkmark rendered in bone off-white (`#EDE8DF`).
- Segmented multi-state toggles resemble flush mechanical switches set into a recessed channel (`#121418`), using tactile sliders (`#282D37`) with fine perimeter framing.
